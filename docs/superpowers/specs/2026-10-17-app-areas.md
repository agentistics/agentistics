## 1. Problem

The app is entering its "corporate" stage and today piles everything into two workspaces behind a
Dashboard|Sessions switch (`components/nav/ModeSwitch.tsx`, `lib/workspaceMode.ts`): a flat list of ten
dashboard links, a header strip carrying a dozen actions, and agent-only information (plan pace, pause,
suggested parallelism from `/api/plan-limits`) that a person cannot see at all. Leadership (leaders,
check-ins, hand-offs, away mode) has no page: it is scattered across chips, settings sections and dialogs.

The owner asked for a side navigation clearly divided by AREAS (reference: MongoDB Atlas), each area its
own page, never everything accumulated on one screen — planned and drawn before implementing.

## 2. Decisions (owner, 2026-10-17 — final)

   search (`Ctrl K`), the areas with attention badges, the ACTIVE area expanded to show its subpages,

3. **Sessions area: "jeito 2" at EVERY width.** Entering Sessões collapses the tree into a 60px icon rail
   (one icon per area, badges kept, a button that reopens the full tree as an overlay), and the existing
   sessions list (`SessionsAside`) becomes its own column (~320px). Column content, top to bottom: title

5. **Page rules.** One area = one page. Fixed page header: area crumb, title, ONE status sentence, at most

6. **Uso › Agora** shows plan pace per ACCOUNT side by side, every harness equal. Harnesses with no

8. HANDOFF.CONFIG (t-49f70edb75) lands in **Liderança › Passagens**. BANG.LIVE (t-015854264f) stays in the

## 3. Area registry — one source of truth

A new pure module `packages/web/src/lib/appAreas.ts` holds the tree: every area, its subpages, the route
each one lives at, its icon key and its PT/EN label. EVERY surface reads it — the desktop tree, the
icon rail, the mobile sheet, the page header crumb, the search, and the route→area resolver. A second list
anywhere (the old `items` array, `navTiles`, `primary`) is the drift CLAUDE.md forbids; the registry test
fails if a route in `AppRouter.tsx` is neither in the registry nor in its explicit `UNLISTED` set.

`areaOfPath(pathname)` → `{ area, page } | null`, matched on segment boundaries (same rule as today's
`modeOfPath`, which it replaces). `modeOfPath` survives as `areaOfPath(p)?.area === 'sessions'` so
existing callers keep compiling during the migration.

## 4. Desktop

### 4.1 The tree (`components/nav/AreaTree.tsx`, new)

Lives in today's `SideNav` `<aside>` (App.tsx ~969–1330) and REPLACES its ModeSwitch + `items` body; the
aside's shell (width 248 / collapsed 64, `AsideHeader`, `AsideResizer`, collapse state
`agentistics-sidebar-collapsed`) is kept. Order: `AsideHeader` (mark + collapse) · search field
(`Ctrl K`, see 4.3) · area rows · footer (today's `footer` block: avatar popover, theme, language, Export,
Ajustes, `PlanUsageFootButton`).

- An area row: icon, label, optional badge (`AttentionDot` style; Sessões = `useFleet().fleet.attention`,
  Uso = number of accounts over pace or on hold, Liderança = check-ins overdue; absent badge when the
  source cannot answer — never a 0 badge). Row height 36px desktop.
- The ACTIVE area (from `areaOfPath`) is expanded showing its subpages (indent 30px, 32px rows, active
  subpage accent left bar). Other areas are collapsed; clicking one navigates to its first subpage (or the
  remembered last subpage of that area, sessionStorage, same pattern ModeSwitch uses today).
- Collapsed aside (64px) shows icons only with `CollapsedTip` tooltips — reuse the local helper.
- `BetaTag` on Agentask as today.

### 4.2 Sessions: rail + column

When `areaOfPath(...).area === 'sessions'` the aside renders `AreaRail` (60px, new, `components/nav/
AreaRail.tsx`): the mark, one icon per area with its badge, and at the bottom a "show tree" button that
opens the full `AreaTree` as an overlay panel over the column (Esc closes; `useEscapeOwner`). Beside the
rail, the sessions column is the EXISTING `SessionsAside` in today's bordered panel with the `PanelGap`
(`ag-gap-aside-left`) resize handle — unchanged component, gains only the column header described in §2.3
(title, the "N precisam de você · M trabalhando" sentence computed from the rows it already has, Nova,
search, Filtros — most of these exist inside SessionsAside today; the work is ORDER and spacing, not new
controls). `Ctrl+B` keeps toggling it (`toggle-left`). `SessionsRail` (the old collapsed body) is
removed once the column is the only shape.

### 4.3 Search (`Ctrl K`)

A field at the top of the tree filters the registry by label (PT and EN, accent-insensitive) and shows
matching subpages; Enter navigates. Built with the existing input styling from `pages/settings/
primitives.tsx` (`FieldInput`), not a new search component. Scope v1: pages only (sessions and tasks are
searched where they live). `Ctrl K` must not fire inside terminals/text fields — reuse
`shouldHandlePanelShortcut`.

### 4.4 The page header (`components/nav/PageHeader.tsx`, new)

Every area page renders it at the top of its content: crumb `Área › Página` (from the registry), title,
one status sentence (passed by the page; may be absent), ≤2 action buttons. The existing `TopBar` strip
keeps its global items (FiltersBar on metrics pages, magnifier, HealthWarnings, Hardware, AwayPill,
VaultHeaderButton, NotificationBell, Live, refresh) — this spec does not move them; it only stops pages
from inventing their own headers. The sessions workspace keeps its `sessionTopBar`.

## 6. The new pages

All pages use existing primitives: `Section`, `StatCard`/`StatTile`, `RecordCard`, `Pill`, `Disclosure`,

### 6.1 Uso

Data: `GET /api/plan-limits` (`plan-limits.ts`, `PlanLimitsPayload`: `limits`, `registered`, `budgets`
with `PlanBudget & PlanPace`, `freeSlots*`, `liveSessions`, `pauseUntil`). The web store
`lib/planLimits.ts` today keeps only `{limits, registered, accountLabels, now}`; it gains `budgets`
(typed rows) — one store, one fetch, refreshed by the existing `plan-limits` SSE signal.

- **Agora** — header sentence summarising pace ("2 de 3 planos acima do ritmo. Não abra sessões novas até
  14:20.") computed by a pure `usageHeadline()`; one card per ACCOUNT (not per harness): plan label, status
  pill (No ritmo / Acima do ritmo / Em pausa from `paceStatus` + `status`), week used vs expected, 5h
  window, pace %/h vs sustainable, parallel now vs suggested, pause until, renews in. Then a "Semana:
  esperado × real" chart and the chip row of harnesses with no registered plan + "Registrar plano"
  (navigates to Ajustes › Billing/accounts). Header actions: "Ajustar limites" (→ Limites), "Planejar
  sessões" (→ Planejar).
- **Planejar** — "I want to work N hours": a pure `planSessions(budgets, hours)` returns per account how many
  parallel sessions fit at the sustainable rate until reset, and the pause times; shown as RecordCards.
  No new server data.
- **Limites** — re-homes `components/nay/NayLimitsTab.tsx` as the page body (the modal `PlanUsageModal`
  keeps wrapping the same component, so the two never drift).
- **Histórico** — expected vs actual over time. The server keeps readings IN MEMORY only (`history` map in
  `plan-limits.ts`, ≤32 readings per key). v1 exposes those readings on the payload (`readings` per
  account) and the page states plainly that history starts when the server started. A durable store is
  OUT of scope (follow-up task).

### 6.4 Uso › Agora — pause and resume at the recommended time (owner, 2026-10-17)

The owner asked for two controls on the usage page: **pause every session and bring it back at the time the
plan pacing recommends**, and **pause only the sessions above the recommended count**, with the return
SCHEDULED so the subscription lasts until it renews. Research of what exists:
`~/.agentistics/leader/app-areas/pause-research.md`. Nothing like it exists today. It is built ENTIRELY out of
three existing mechanisms; there is no second way to stop or reopen a session.

**What it is built from (reuse, never a copy).**
- *Stopping* = the existing **graceful retire** (`ManagedSession.retire`, `planRetireRequest`, run by
  `sessions/retire-runner.ts`, decided by the pure `decideRetire`): the session ends only after its turn is
  over and it has been quiet (`waiting` on two polls, no background task, no child command, hand-off successor
  alive). It never cuts a `working` or `waiting-approval` session. The pause sets the retire deadline to the
  RESUME time (not the 30-minute default): a session still busy when the resume time comes was never paused
  and simply keeps running.
- *Resuming* = the existing **reopen** (`host.resumeSession` → `resumeSessionLocked`, `reopen-target.ts`,
  `reopen-inherit.ts`): same conversation by id, same task/subtask, label, note, account (re-validated), model
  and effort, role, pin, folder, notify state and check-in agenda.
- *When* = `budgets[i].pauseUntil` and `budgets[i].suggestedParallel` of the session's ACCOUNT from
  `/api/plan-limits` (per account — never the top-level `pauseUntil`, which is the earliest of all).

**New pieces.**
1. **The pause record** — `ManagedSession.pause?: { resumeAt: number; account: string; scope: 'all' | 'excess';
   createdAt: number; mode?: string }`, written on the row together with its `retire` request, in the registry
   (`managed-sessions.json`), so it survives a server restart and a reboot. `mode` is the permission mode the
   row showed at pause time, because a reopen does not carry it (re-applied with `setMode` where the row
   supports it; otherwise said in the resume notification).
2. **The selection rule** — pure `sessions/pause-plan.ts`:
   - `scope: 'all'`: every running managed session whose account HAS a `pauseUntil` in the future. Sessions on
     accounts that are on pace keep running and are listed as such ("na conta X o ritmo está bom; ficam
     rodando").
   - `scope: 'excess'` (per account): `live(account) − suggestedParallel` sessions, **workers only**, chosen
     by least recent user activity first, then newest spawn. Resume time = that account's `pauseUntil`, or when
     the account has none, the next 5-hour reset.
   - **Skipped, each with a sentence** (never silently): `waiting-approval` (a person must answer first), a row
     with `handoffTo`/`handoffRelayAwaiting` (mid hand-off), a row with a retire already pending, `external`
     rows (not ours), `opencode` (cannot be reopened), a gemini row with a synthetic id (cannot resume by id), a
     row with no `conversationId` (a reopen could not name it).
   - "All" includes leaders; leaders are scheduled to resume FIRST.
   - A row's ACCOUNT is its `ManagedSession.accountId`; with none, the harness's single bound account; when the
     harness has several accounts and the row names none, the row is skipped ("não sei de qual conta ela gasta")
     — a session is never paused against a guessed account.
3. **The resume runner** — `sessions/pause-runner.ts`, a tick (30 s) started beside `startRetireRunner`
   in the server process only. For every ENDED row with a `pause` whose `resumeAt <= now`: re-read plan limits
   for that account (the time can move); if the account is on hold or still over pace, POSTPONE to the new
   `pauseUntil` (notify once); otherwise reopen up to that account's `suggestedParallel` at this moment (leaders
   first, then in pause order) and postpone the rest by one 5-hour window. The reopen carries a short prompt so
   the session knows why it is back: "[agentistics] Retomando após pausa para respeitar o limite do plano.
   Continue de onde parou." A refused reopen (memory gate, account refused, conversation held by another row)
   is retried on the next tick, at most 10 times, then dropped with a notification naming the session. At boot,
   anything already due fires after the first fleet poll. A row whose retire EXPIRED (it never got quiet) has
   its pause record dropped — it was never stopped.
4. **Cancel / resume now** — per session and for all: "Retomar agora" (reopen immediately, ignoring the
   schedule — the person decided) and "Cancelar retomada" (stays stopped). A person reopening a paused session
   by hand clears its record. A `retire --cancel` clears it too.
5. **Routes** under the existing `/api/fleet` prefix (so `capability-guard.ts` already gates them as
   `localShell`): `POST /api/fleet/pause` `{ scope, account?, preview? }` → with `preview: true` returns the
   plan (who pauses, who is skipped and why, resume time per account) without writing; without it, writes;
   `POST /api/fleet/pause/cancel` `{ ids? }`; `POST /api/fleet/pause/resume-now` `{ ids? }`;
   `GET /api/fleet/paused` lists the records. The fleet row gains `pausedUntil?: number` so every surface can
   show it.
6. **Notifications** through the existing `broadcastNotification` codes (localized at render time):
   `sessions.paused` (N sessions will stop at the end of their turn and come back at HH:MM),
   `sessions.resumed`, `sessions.resume_postponed`, `sessions.resume_failed`. While the owner is away, the
   same text also goes as an away notice.

**The screen (Uso › Agora).** The page header's two actions become the pause actions WHEN an account is over
pace or on hold: "Pausar tudo e voltar às HH:MM" (primary) and "Ajustar limites"; otherwise they stay as in
§6.1. Each account card over its recommended count shows a line "3 em paralelo · recomendado 1" with the
button "Pausar as 2 a mais". Every pause opens a `ConfirmModal` built from the `preview` answer: who stops
(title, harness · model, "termina a vez dela e para"), who is skipped and why, who keeps running, the resume
time, and the one thing that is lost (text typed in a composer and not sent). A "Pausadas" `Section` lists the
paused sessions with "volta às HH:MM" and the per-row/all "Retomar agora" / "Cancelar retomada". In the
sessions column a paused row carries a chip "pausada · volta 14:20" (existing `Pill`, muted tone). Mobile: the
same buttons and modal (full screen), the list as `RecordCard`s.

**Out of scope here.** An MCP tool so a leader can trigger the pause itself (the route is shaped for it; adding
the tool is a follow-up); pausing sessions on another machine; a "stop now, cutting the turn" option.

## 8. Equal harnesses, i18n, accessibility

- Every per-harness surface iterates `SURFACE_HARNESS_ORDER`; Uso cards are per account and every harness
  with a plan gets the same card. No Claude-first ordering.
- Labels live in the registry (`labelPt`/`labelEn`), matching the repo's inline-ternary convention.
- The tree is a `nav` with `aria-current="page"` on the active subpage; the mobile sheet traps focus and
  closes on Esc / backdrop. `headerHostsMagnifier` keeps its meaning (the mobile bar is chrome).

## 9. Out of scope

actions; global search over sessions/tasks; anything central/members; the cockpit (TUI) and the VS Code
extension.
